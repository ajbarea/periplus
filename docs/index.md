What is Periplus?
{ .section-eyebrow }

## A mariner's log, rebuilt for signal dead zones

A *periplus* was the log of ports along a voyage. This one is a web app: open it once on signal and the whole itinerary keeps working where the coverage drops.
{ .section-lead }

</div>
</section>

<div class="section-inner" markdown>

Built for the road
{ .section-eyebrow }

## Everything you need, with no bars

<div class="feature-grid" markdown>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>

### :material-wifi-off: Works offline

Open it once and the whole trip is cached. Airplane mode, mountain passes, dead zones: it still loads.

</div>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>

### :material-cellphone-arrow-down: Installs like an app

Add it to your Home Screen on iOS or Android: its own icon, full screen, no browser bar.

</div>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>

### :material-map-marker-path: Tap to navigate

Every spot has a Directions link that opens Google or Apple Maps, whichever you picked.

</div>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>

### :material-calendar-plus: Add to calendar

Every event opens in Google Calendar or saves to Apple Calendar, made on your phone.

</div>

</div>

</div>
</section>

Fork it

## Make the trip yours, no code

<div class="steps" markdown>

<div class="step" markdown>

### Edit in the browser

Tap **Edit** and change any text; add, reorder or remove spots, weeks and checklists.

</div>

<div class="step" markdown>

### Export it

**Export** copies the whole trip as JSON, on your device.

</div>

<div class="step" markdown>

### Drop it in your fork

Paste it over the `TRIP` block in your fork, commit, and your trip deploys.

</div>

</div>
